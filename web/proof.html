Proof

Check every claim on chain.

Each requirement of the Post-Quantum dApp category, and where to check it.

pqcVerify on chain

release and every bid read the member's key from pqcKeyStatus (0x…0A02) and verify a 4,627-byte ML-DSA-87 signature with pqcVerify (0x…0A01). A real payout.

PqcGate.sol ↗

Real signatures, live precompile

Real ML-DSA-87 keys and signatures sent to the live pqcVerify: the valid one is accepted; a changed message, a changed signature, another key's signature and a short signature are all refused.

PQC_VECTORS.md ↗

Post-quantum key to join

join only accepts accounts with a registered ML-DSA-87 key and proof of possession.

RoscaCircle.sol ↗

Scoped EVM window

Each member's everyday window reaches only their circle, signed hybrid with their post-quantum key; the fUSD approval gets its own one-transaction window. A circle-only window.

actions.ts ↗

A stolen classical key moves nothing

After each payout the keeper tries, with the winner's classical key only, an fUSD transfer and a native send. The chain refuses both before they reach a block, in its own words.

Security page ↗

Foundry tests

73 unit, fuzz and invariant tests: a stolen classical key can't redirect the pot, the organiser can't take it, and money is conserved over 128,000 random actions.

Live transactions

The factory, the first full round and every circle the keeper runs, with explorer links.

PROOF.md ↗

Two-stage QCAI audit

Before fixes: 72/100, high risk. After fixes, on the QoreChain setting: 78/100, medium risk, rated quantum-safe. Every finding is answered with code and tests.
