Run a round of Rosca yourself.

Make a wallet with a post-quantum key, open a circle with two bots, pay in, try to steal the pot, then sign the payout with ML-DSA-87 and watch the chain check it.

Everything is signed in this browser. Keys are kept in this browser's storage and never sent anywhere. Testnet only.

  1. 1

    Make your wallet

    A fresh test wallet is made in this browser: a secp256k1 key for ordinary transactions and an ML-DSA-87 post-quantum key for payouts. Both stay in this browser only.

  2. 2

    Get test money

    Rosca's faucet sends 2 test QOR for gas and 40 fUSD, the fest's test dollar. Neither has any value.

  3. 3

    Register your post-quantum key

    Your ML-DSA-87 public key goes on chain with a proof that you hold the secret key. Circles only accept members who did this.

  4. 4

    Open your circle

    A circle for three: you and two bots run by Rosca. You open a window scoped to the factory, create the circle, then a window scoped to only your circle and fUSD, and join first.

  5. 5

    Pay round 1, and bid if you like

    Everyone pays $10. Members who need the money now bid the discount they accept. The bots don't bid, and you joined first, so the pot is yours either way. A discount you bid is shared out to everyone.

  6. 6

    Close the auction and approve

    Bidding lasts two minutes. Then anyone can close the auction, and a majority (2 of 3) must approve the payout. One bot approves at once; the second vote can be yours.

  7. 7

    Be the thief

    Try to send the pot to a stranger instead of yourself, reusing your own real signature. The signature names the recipient, so the contract refuses it on chain.

  8. 8

    Sign the payout and take the pot

    Your browser signs the payout (circle, round, recipient, amount, deadline) with your ML-DSA-87 key. The contract checks it with pqcVerify and pays you.

  9. 9

    Finish the circle

    The bots win rounds 2 and 3. Pay them if you like; if you don't, the security held back from your payout covers you. When the circle is complete, take back what is yours.
