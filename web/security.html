Security

Who can move the pot?

Only the winner and a majority of members, together. Nobody can do it alone: not the organiser, not a thief, not a quantum computer.

✕

The organiser

Opens the circle and nothing else. The contract has no owner, no admin, no upgrade and no withdraw function.

✕

A thief with a member's wallet key

A stolen secp256k1 key cannot make an ML-DSA-87 signature. Each member's EVM window is also scoped to this circle and fUSD, and only their post-quantum key can open a wider one.

✕

A future quantum computer

It could recover an ECDSA key from its public key. It cannot forge ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204, security level 5).

✓

The winner, with a majority

The payout needs most members' approvals and the winner's ML-DSA-87 signature over this circle, this round, the recipient, the amount and a deadline.

The thief test, run for real

Once per circle the keeper plays a thief who has stolen the winner's classical key. The thief sends release to their own address, either with a signature from their own ML-DSA-87 key or with the winner's real signature replayed for another recipient. Every attempt lands on chain and fails.

  • The first attempt runs in round 2 of the live circle.

Try to steal a pot

Start a circle in the app, win a round, and play the thief against your own pot. The chain refuses it.
